# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This repository supports a training course, and people come to it with very different levels of experience. We want everyone who uses it, asks a question about it or contributes to it to be treated with respect, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient with questions, including basic ones. Everyone was new to this once.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback in the same way.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Admit mistakes, fix them and move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Mocking someone for what they don't know
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, in the training sessions that use this material, and wherever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainer, [@iomegak12](https://github.com/iomegak12) on GitHub. During a training session you can also speak to the instructor directly.

All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on the seriousness and whether it is repeated, the response may be a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
